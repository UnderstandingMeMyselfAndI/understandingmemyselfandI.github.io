Privacy Notice

Last updated: [add date]

1. Who we are

This app is a local-only web application (PWA).
We do not operate a server, collect analytics, or receive any personal data from you.

For the purposes of data protection law:

You are the data controller

This app does not act as a data processor

2. What data this app stores

This app stores data only on your own device.
Typical data may include:

Information you enter into the app (for example, logs, notes, or preferences)

App settings (such as reminders or display options)

Optional security information (such as an encrypted lock)

No data is sent to us or any third party.

3. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored locally using:

Your browser’s secure storage (IndexedDB / local storage)

Your device’s own file system (via your browser)

We do not store or receive your data.
We cannot see, access, or recover it.

4. Encryption and security

If you choose to enable security features:

Your data is encrypted on your device using your password

The password is never sent anywhere

We do not know or store your password

If the correct password is not entered, the data cannot be read.

⚠️ If you forget your password and any recovery options you set, your data cannot be recovered.

5. PINs and convenience access

You may optionally set a PIN for quicker access:

PINs are stored securely on your device

They are never stored as plain numbers

PINs are used only to unlock access on your device

6. Data sharing

We do not:

Share data with third parties

Use analytics or tracking

Send data to servers

Use cookies for profiling or advertising

Your data stays on your device at all times.

7. Your rights under GDPR

Because this app stores data only on your device, your GDPR rights are exercised directly by you:

Right of access – you can view your data in the app

Right to rectification – you can edit your data

Right to erasure – you can delete your data

Right to portability – you can export your data (if export is enabled)

There is no data held by us to request, correct, or delete on your behalf.

8. Data retention

Your data remains on your device until:

You delete it in the app

You clear your browser storage

You uninstall the app or reset your device

We do not retain any copies.

9. Children’s data

This app does not knowingly collect data from children.
All data entry is entirely user-controlled.

10. Changes to this notice

If this privacy notice changes, the updated version will be shown within the app.

11. Contact

Because this app does not collect or receive personal data, there is no data controller contact address for data access requests.

If you have general questions about how the app works, you can refer to the help section within the app.
